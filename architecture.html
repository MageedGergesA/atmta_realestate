Overview

A seven-module Odoo 18 suite covering the full real-estate lifecycle: rental operations, sales brokerage, off-plan development, construction tracking, unit handover, financial feasibility analysis, and a customer-facing portal.

ModulePurpose
atmta_real_estateFoundation — rental lifecycle (properties, contracts, payments, deposits)
real_estate_brokerageSales pipeline (listings, leads, viewings, offers, commissions)
real_estate_developerOff-plan sales (projects, phases, reservations, installments)
real_estate_constructionBuild tracking (milestones with dependencies, sub-tasks, contractors, cost vs budget, progress billing with retention)
real_estate_handoverUnit delivery (checklists, snagging, warranties)
real_estate_investmentFinancial appraisal (NPV, IRR, payback, scenarios)
real_estate_portalCustomer-facing self-service portal (views/controllers only)

Sales Pipeline — 7 Stages

The brokerage pipeline moves a property from the seller's hands into the buyer's, through seven well-defined stages. Each stage owns a distinct model (Listing → Lead → Viewing → Offer → Transaction → Commission) and has its own state machine. Forward arrows mean conversion; backward / counter arrows are negotiations within a stage.
